CANCELLATION POLICY

1. ORDER CANCELLATION WINDOW
Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement if the order has not yet shipped. After 24 hours, cancellation is not possible and you must use the return/refund process instead.

2. HOW TO CANCEL
To cancel an order:
1. Log into your account and go to "My Orders"
2. Click "Cancel Order" on the order you wish to cancel
3. Select a reason for cancellation
4. Confirm the cancellation

Alternatively, contact customer service within 24 hours with your order number.

3. CANCELLATION CONFIRMATION
Once an order is successfully cancelled, you will receive a confirmation email. Any payment will be refunded to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.

4. PARTIAL CANCELLATION
If you ordered multiple items, you can cancel individual items from your order. The refund will be prorated based on the item price.

5. ITEMS ALREADY SHIPPED
Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled. You must refuse delivery or initiate a return once received.

6. SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES
- Pre-order items: Can be cancelled anytime before the item ships
- Backorder items: Can be cancelled anytime
- Gift cards: Cannot be cancelled once purchased

7. CANCELLATION FEES
No cancellation fees apply. However, if you cancel after the 24-hour window, you will need to use the return process and may be responsible for return shipping costs.

8. SUBSCRIPTION ORDERS
Subscription orders can be cancelled anytime. The cancellation takes effect on the next billing cycle. No refunds are issued for the current billing period.
