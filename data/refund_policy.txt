REFUND POLICY

1. REFUND ELIGIBILITY
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for most items. Refunds are available for:
- Unopened, unused items in original packaging
- Items with manufacturing defects
- Items that do not match the product description

2. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS
The following items cannot be refunded:
- Clearance or final sale items (marked as such at purchase)
- Digital products or software licenses once downloaded
- Custom or personalized items
- Items purchased more than 30 days ago
- Items that show signs of use or damage caused by the customer

3. REFUND PROCESS
To request a refund:
1. Contact customer service with your order number and reason for refund
2. Provide photos if claiming a defect
3. Ship the item back to us (prepaid label provided for defective items)
4. Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the returned item

4. REFUND AMOUNT
- Full refund of purchase price for eligible items
- Original shipping costs are non-refundable
- Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item is defective

5. SPECIAL CASES
- Defective items: Full refund including original shipping
- Wrong item sent: Full refund and free return shipping
- Items damaged in transit: Full refund if reported within 48 hours of delivery
