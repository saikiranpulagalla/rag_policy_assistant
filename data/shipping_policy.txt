SHIPPING POLICY

1. SHIPPING METHODS
We offer the following shipping options:
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days, FREE on orders over $50
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days, $9.99
- Overnight Shipping: Next business day, $24.99
- International Shipping: 10-15 business days, calculated at checkout

2. PROCESSING TIME
All orders are processed within 1-2 business days before shipment. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.

3. SHIPPING COSTS
- Orders under $50: $5.99 standard shipping
- Orders $50-$100: FREE standard shipping
- Orders over $100: FREE standard shipping with free express upgrade
- International orders: Shipping cost varies by destination

4. DELIVERY GUARANTEES
- Standard shipping: Guaranteed delivery within 7 business days or money back
- Express shipping: Guaranteed delivery within 3 business days or $10 credit
- Overnight shipping: Guaranteed next business day delivery or full refund

5. TRACKING
All shipments include tracking numbers sent via email. Customers can track their order on our website using the tracking number.

6. LOST OR DAMAGED SHIPMENTS
- Report lost packages within 30 days of delivery date
- Report damaged packages within 48 hours of delivery
- We will investigate and either reship or issue a full refund

7. RESTRICTED AREAS
We do not ship to:
- PO Boxes (except for specific items)
- Military addresses (use APO/FPO addresses instead)
- Certain international countries due to customs restrictions
